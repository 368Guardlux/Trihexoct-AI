# Trihexoct-AI

Trihexoct AI is a super-strong lock for your internet browser that completely stops websites from watching what you do, learning who you are, or looking at your computer parts. 
When you visit a normal website, that site usually tries to leave tiny hidden trackers on your computer to remember you, checks what kind of screen or speakers you have, and even watches how fast you move your mouse or type on your keyboard so they can build a detailed profile about your habits.
Trihexoct AI steps in and aggressively blocks all of those tricks by acting like a heavy steel door between you and the internet. 
If a website tries to peek at your saved files, use your computer's extra graphics power, check your internet connection speed, map out your Bluetooth devices, or see what other tabs you have open, this system just gives them a fake, blank answer or completely shuts them down instantly. 
It also cuts the secret, invisible wires that big advertising companies use to talk to each other in the background, making sure your personal information can never be shared or saved anywhere. 
It even stops modern websites from knowing when you look away from the screen or put your computer to sleep. But because this shield is so incredibly strict and breaks all the normal rules that websites expect you to follow, it ends up breaking a lot of the internet for you, too. For example, if you use this shield, you will not be able to watch protected movies on places like Netflix, many interactive buttons on websites will simply stop working, and pages might load as a totally blank white screen because they cannot handle being told "no" so many times. It completely deletes your ability to stay logged in, save your place in a web app, or keep your settings for later. 
Ultimately, it is not a tool for normal, everyday web surfing; instead, it is an extreme, special defense built only for situations where someone is willing to completely break how websites work just to guarantee that they remain an absolute, untraceable ghost while they are online.
